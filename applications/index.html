01

Flooring

Light-filled and lasting. Choose honed or brushed for kitchens, entrances and bathrooms.

02

Wall cladding

Any finish works on walls. Confirm the fixing method with your installer.

03

Kitchens

Beautiful, but acids such as lemon and vinegar can dull the surface. A honed finish hides marks best.

04

Bathrooms

Marble's natural home. Use honed or textured finishes underfoot for grip.

05

Staircases

Plan for slip resistance and choose treads and risers from the same lot.

06

Feature walls

Book-matched slabs, cut in sequence from one block, create a mirrored pattern.

07

Commercial & hospitality

For lobbies, hotels and residences. Agree quantities and reference slabs early.
