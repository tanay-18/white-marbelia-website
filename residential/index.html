Which white is yours?

  • Soft and warm. Namibia White

Room by room

Every slab is unique, so we show you the actual stone first. All our marble is 20 mm, cut to the size of your rooms.

Kitchen

Lemon, vinegar and wine can dull marble. A honed finish hides marks best; seal it and wipe spills quickly.

Bathroom

Any finish works on walls and vanities. Use honed or textured stone on the floor for grip.

Floors

Calm whites such as Vietnam White or Greek Thassos keep large floors light and even.

Feature walls

Italian Statuario makes the statement. Book-matched slabs mirror the veining across the wall.
