Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information White Marbelia collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We have kept it short and in plain language.

Who we are

This website is operated by White Marbelia, a natural-stone business with offices in:

  • India: 212/2, Koppa Gate, Koppa Village, Jigni Hobli, Anekal Taluk, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560105
  • Vietnam: 254 Truong Chinh Street, Dong Hung Thuan Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

For any question about your personal information, or to make a request, email whitemarbeliavn@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

When you send an enquiry, we collect what you type into the form. Depending on the form, that is your name, email address, phone number (optional), company, role, country or city, the stones you are interested in, quantities, timelines and your message. We also note which page the enquiry came from and the time you ticked the consent box.

We use this only to reply to your enquiry, prepare information or a quotation you ask for, and keep a record of our conversation with you. We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

To protect the forms from spam, the website briefly keeps a scrambled (one-way encrypted) version of your internet address for 10 minutes to stop repeated automated submissions. We cannot recover your actual address from it.

If you message us on WhatsApp or call us, we receive the messages and details you choose to share.

We do not use advertising trackers. If we add website statistics (such as Google Analytics) in future, they will only switch on after you agree through a cookie notice, and we will update this policy first.

Where your information goes

  • Our email: enquiries are delivered to our business inbox, which is provided by Google (Gmail). Google stores and processes these emails on our behalf.
  • Our website host: the company that hosts this website stores the site and, if we keep a backup copy of an enquiry, that copy.
  • WhatsApp: if you choose to contact us there, WhatsApp (Meta) handles that conversation under its own privacy policy.

Because we have offices in India and Vietnam, serve clients worldwide, and our email and hosting providers operate internationally, your information may be handled in countries other than your own. We only share it with the services above, and only so that we can respond to you.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any business that follows, and then delete them. Backup copies of enquiries kept on the website are deleted automatically after 180 days.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop using it (you can withdraw the consent you gave when you sent the form).

Email whitemarbeliavn@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as we can, and within the time required by the law that applies to you. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may also contact the data protection authority in your country.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. Your browser may store a small setting so that pages work properly, and website administrators receive login cookies when they sign in. If statistics cookies are ever added, you will be asked first and can change your choice at any time.

Children

This website is intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
